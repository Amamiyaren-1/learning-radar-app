# Audio source and reuse

`frontend/public/audio/atmosphere.ogg` is unchanged from LBEILC/RhineLabUI. Upstream `public/audio/README.md` describes original programmatic composition without film samples, external recordings, or voices, licensed under its MIT notice. Only the atmosphere layer is included; no upstream MP3, other stems, or sampled typing sounds are shipped.

Project `click.wav`, `switch.wav`, `open.wav`, and `back.wav` are generated by make-ui-sounds.py: 24kHz, mono, 16-bit, 55/85/180/110ms. They use generated tones/noise and no recordings. Original-code licensing is pending owner approval.

Existing playback behavior remains: new visitors start enabled (music 18%, effects 28%), an explicit disabled choice persists, and browser autoplay denial waits for a valid user gesture. One AudioContext, one loop, bounded short effects, and 80ms global throttling are retained. Reading the same scene keeps the player; session progress contains no business/authentication data. Decode/playback state does not prove a human listened.

The game-film short samples in upstream are outside its original-music grant and are absent. See THIRD-PARTY.md; code/audio permissions do not resolve game imagery or model design rights.
