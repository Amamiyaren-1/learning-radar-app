# Public development scope

The owner-approved original frontend is the sole visual baseline. Preserve its
archive model, brand contour, derived artwork, materials, lighting, camera,
layout and motion. Generic substitute assets and their dedicated tests are
withdrawn. Do not regenerate a substitute or beautify the baseline. See
docs/PUBLIC-VISUALS.md. README images may be refreshed from the restored actual
fixture pages as authorized. Commercial font binaries remain excluded.

Use isolated fixture accounts/data and offline adapters. Real models, project collection, and SMTP require explicit authorization. Preserve native authentication/CSRF, durable queues/budgets, quality gates, stable identities, and Chinese product behavior.

Commit titles and bodies must be English: `type(scope): short English summary`, aiming for 50–72 characters where useful. Include reasons and meaningful verification in the body. Explain progress to the owner in Chinese. Use confirmed public Git identities; preserve export attribution and honest chronology.

Developer docs, comments, and docstrings are English. Chinese interface/report/prompt strings and test inputs/expectations stay Chinese. Preserve third-party notices verbatim.

Never commit private profiles, account verifiers, session/CSRF values, databases, reports, logs, conversations, or commercial fonts. Public screenshots contain visibly marked synthetic data; fixtures never prove real integration.

Preserve public English docs, licenses, notices, the accepted original visual baseline, and manifests during allowlist sync. Never merge/mirror private history. Follow docs/SOURCE-SYNC.md. Remote creation, push, visibility change, deployment, and video upload require explicit release authorization naming SHA/parameters. If a resource has a specific distribution restriction, list its exact path, evidence and minimum suggested action; do not automatically replace visuals.
