# Public source provenance and scope

The public line has independent sanitized export history, never private development history. Keep EXPORT-MANIFEST.json as the historical baseline, and FINAL-SOURCE-MANIFEST.json as the current file/hash and allowed-difference record. Local privacy evidence stays outside public source.

Use README.md or README.en.md for installation; DEVELOPER-GUIDE.md for architecture, fixture checks, authentication, budgets, and networking; THIRD-PARTY.md for exact material treatment. SOURCE-SYNC.md protects public English docs, licenses, assets, and history during future updates.

The Chinese product interface, report/prompt templates, and test fixtures remain unchanged. Public developer prose/comments are English. Generic archive-card/loop assets and commercial-font fallback are the documented public visual differences. Original-code MIT is still a proposal, not an effective license.

No real business calls are implied by fixture acceptance. Structural success does not establish semantic correctness or actual project execution. Public HTTPS and Linux deployment require separate acceptance. Publication needs explicit owner/repository, license, and final SHA authorization.
